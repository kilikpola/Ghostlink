Home/Setup guide

Set up GhostLink in seven steps.

From download to your first end-to-end encrypted message — no account, no email, no phone number. About five minutes, most of it spent writing down twelve words.

The walkthrough

Zero to encrypted, step by step.

Choose your platform

GhostLink runs wherever you do. Pick one — you can add the others later, your identity travels with your seed phrase.

  • Web app — nothing to install, runs in any modern browser.
  • Android APK — download from GitHub releases and sideload (Android 7.0+).
  • Desktop — Windows, macOS and Linux builds are on the way.

First launch — your key is born

The first time GhostLink runs it generates a private key on your device. That key is your identity: there is no signup form, no username, no password.

Your key never leaves your device. Not to us — there is no "us" in the middle — and not to anyone else.

Back up your seed phrase

GhostLink shows you a 12-word recovery phrase (BIP39). Write it down on paper and store it somewhere safe — not in a screenshot, not in a cloud note.

This phrase is the only way to recover your identity if the device is lost or wiped. No one can recover it for you — that is the point. Lose it, and the identity is gone forever.

Invite a peer

To talk to someone, exchange an invite: a short code or a QR code. Share it in person, or over any channel you already have — email, another messenger, a piece of paper.

When they accept, your devices perform an ECDH P-256 key exchange directly with each other and open an encrypted peer-to-peer link. No server ever sees the keys.

Verify the fingerprint

Before trusting the link, compare the peer's security fingerprint: a short code derived from their public key, shown in the conversation details.

Read it out over a call or compare in person. If both sides match, no one is sitting between you. This takes ten seconds and only needs doing once per peer.

Send your first encrypted message

Type and hit send. Your words are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device and travel straight to your peer — ciphertext on the wire, plaintext only at the two ends.

Try the live cipher demo on the home page to see what an eavesdropper would actually read.

Optional: vanish and share

Two extras worth knowing from day one:

  • Self-destruct — set messages to cryptographically delete themselves after 30s, 60s or 5 minutes.
  • File transfer — send files as encrypted 64KB chunks, SHA-256 verified on arrival.

That's the whole setup. Add more peers the same way — invite, verify, talk. Reconnecting is automatic once a peer is known.

Good to know

Tips before you disappear.

1
Paper beats pixels for your seed phrase

A photo or cloud note of your 12 words syncs to places you can't see. Ink on paper in a drawer has no attack surface.

2
Always verify fingerprints out of band

Comparing fingerprints over the same channel you invited on proves nothing. Use a second channel, or do it in person.

3
Offline peers aren't lost peers

Messages queue locally for up to 24 hours and deliver when your peer comes back online. No server stores them in between.

4
One identity, many devices

Restore the same seed phrase on your phone and desktop to carry your identity across platforms.
